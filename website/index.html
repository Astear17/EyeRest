Local by design

Your phone keeps the time.

EyeRest will schedule notifications directly on your device. You choose each interval, message, and quiet period; the app reconciles schedules whenever preferences change or it returns to the foreground.

  • Independent, adjustable reminder categories
  • Snooze and pause controls when life gets busy
  • Notification batches designed around iOS limits
  • Absolute-time countdowns that account for time-zone changes

A native foundation

Prepared for the iPhone in your hand.

EyeRest is planned as a SwiftUI app for iPhone XS and newer, with iOS 17 as its deployment target. It will use native local notifications and on-device storage—no third-party runtime dependencies.
