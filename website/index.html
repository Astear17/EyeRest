Questions, answered

A little more
peace of mind.

Will EyeRest work without an internet connection?+

That is the intended experience. The app is designed around local notifications and on-device preferences, rather than an account or cloud connection.

Can I choose which reminders I receive?+

Yes. The planned app includes independent controls for eye relaxation, hydration, blinking, posture, and movement, with adjustable intervals and messages.

Can I pause reminders when I need focus?+

Yes. The planned home screen includes a quick pause and resume control, plus snooze actions for individual reminders.

Which iPhones will be supported?+

EyeRest is planned for iPhone XS and newer, with iOS 17.0 as the minimum target.

When can I download EyeRest?+

There is no public download yet. The app is in development and has not been released on the App Store or as a public device build.
